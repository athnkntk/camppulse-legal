CAMPPULSE

The camping app that works where your signal doesn't.

Offline plans, checklists, downloadable maps and safety tools that keep working at zero bars — plus night sound awareness and an encrypted camp mesh no one else ships.

Available on the App Store

Built for the last place your phone works

Offline-first planning

Camp plans, gear checklists, saved places and packing templates — all on-device, all available with no connection.

Maps without a signal

Download map areas before you leave. Navigate and find your way back to camp entirely offline.

Check-in timer

Promise a return time. If you miss it, your location and note are queued and delivered when connectivity returns.

Night sound awareness

On-device audio classification warns about animals, people and vehicles near your camp — audio never leaves the phone.

Encrypted camp mesh

Device-to-device group messaging between nearby iPhones over Bluetooth/Wi-Fi. No internet, no relay.

Honest SOS

SOS packets queue on-device with delivery proof. CampPulse never claims to contact rescue services — your safety, told straight.

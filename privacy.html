CampPulse Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

On this device

Plans, checklists, saved places and packing lists are stored only on your device.

Account

Your email and an installation identifier are stored on our server. Your password is stored only as a hash.

SOS alerts

An SOS uploads the location and message you choose and links them to your account. CampPulse never contacts rescue services automatically.

Maps

Online maps and map downloads send your IP address and the requested area to the map provider.

Payments

Apple processes payments. CampPulse verifies the purchase signature and links it to your user id; CampPulse never sees card details.

Microphone

The Sound Radar page classifies ambient audio on-device using Apple's built-in classifier. Audio is never recorded, stored, or sent off the device.

Local network

Camp Group uses MultipeerConnectivity (Bluetooth/Wi-Fi) for encrypted device-to-device messaging with nearby devices you approve. Nothing is relayed to the internet.

Tracking

No advertising, no ad trackers.

Your controls

Export or clear local planning data anytime. Deleting your account removes your server-side account and SOS data.

Contact

Privacy questions: GitHub Issues
